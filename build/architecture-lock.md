## 2. Frozen Sign Convention, Symbol & Math Contract

### 2.1 First Law Sign Convention (Physics Locked; Chemistry Cross-Referenced)
* **Primary Binder Convention (Physics / Engineering):**
  $$\Delta U = Q - W_{\mathrm{by}}, \qquad W_{\mathrm{by}} = \int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,dV$$
  * $Q > 0$: heat transferred **into** the system from surroundings.
  * $Q < 0$: heat rejected **out of** the system to surroundings.
  * $W_{\mathrm{by}} > 0$: work done **by** the system on surroundings (gas expansion, $\Delta V > 0$).
  * $W_{\mathrm{by}} < 0$: work done **on** the system by surroundings (gas compression, $\Delta V < 0$).
* **Chemistry Convention Translation (Explicitly Boxed in §§1, 2, 7, 8):**
  $$\Delta U = q + w_{\mathrm{on}}, \qquad w_{\mathrm{on}} = -W_{\mathrm{by}} = -\int_{V_1}^{V_2} P_{\mathrm{ext}}\,dV$$
* **Cycle Magnitude Convention (§9):** In cycle efficiency and COP formulas, $Q_H = |Q_{\mathrm{in}}| > 0$, $Q_C = |Q_{\mathrm{out}}| > 0$, and $W_{\mathrm{net}} = Q_H - Q_C > 0$ (engine) or $W_{\mathrm{in}} = Q_H - Q_C > 0$ (refrigerator/heat pump).

### 2.2 Locked Symbol Dictionary
* $U, H, F, G, S$: total internal energy $[\si{\joule}]$, enthalpy $[\si{\joule}]$, Helmholtz free energy $[\si{\joule}]$, Gibbs free energy $[\si{\joule}]$, entropy $[\si{\joule\per\kelvin}]$.
* $u, h, f, g, s$: mass-specific quantities $[\si{\joule\per\kilogram}]$ or $[\si{\joule\per\kilogram\per\kelvin}]$.
* $U_m, H_m, S_m, C_{V,m}, C_{P,m}$: molar quantities $[\si{\joule\per\mole}]$ or $[\si{\joule\per\mole\per\kelvin}]$.
* $c, c_p, c_v$: specific heat capacities $[\si{\joule\per\kilogram\per\kelvin}]$ or $[\si{\joule\per\gram\per\kelvin}]$; $C = mc = n C_m$: total heat capacity $[\si{\joule\per\kelvin}]$.
* $L_f, L_v, L_s$ (or $\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{vap}}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{sub}}$): latent heats of fusion, vaporization, sublimation $[\si{\joule\per\kilogram}]$ or $[\si{\kilojoule\per\kilogram}]$.
* $Q$ $[\si{\joule}]$ vs. $\dot{Q}$ $[\si{\watt}]$ vs. $q'' = \dot{Q}/A$ $[\si{\watt\per\square\meter}]$: total heat energy, heat transfer rate (power), and heat flux. (Every heat-transfer rate in §§10–16 uses $\dot{Q}$ or explicit $[\si{\watt}]$ units so energy $[\si{\joule}]$ and power $[\si{\watt}]$ are never confused.)
* $R_{\mathrm{th}}$ $[\si{\kelvin\per\watt}]$ vs. $R_{\mathrm{val}} = L/k$ $[\si{\square\meter\kelvin\per\watt}]$: lumped thermal resistance vs. unit-area thermal $R$-value.

### 2.3 Math Rule `5.d.iv` Compliance Contract ("No Trigonometry or Calculus Required")
* Every equation in §§0–23 must be written in **closed algebraic form** using arithmetic, ratios, powers/exponents, roots, absolute values, $\ln$, $\log_{10}$, and $e^x$ (all permitted under Rule `5.d.i–iii` and `MathGuidelines2022.pdf` Division C Algebra II).

## 5. Verification Infrastructure Contract (`verify/verify_all.py`)

* `verify/verify_all.py` auto-discovers every module matching `verify/checks/check_*.py` (or `verify/checks/sec*.py`).
* Each check module defines `CHECKS: list[tuple[str, Callable[[], tuple[bool, str]], str]]` (or a `run_checks()` function returning a list of check dictionaries `{id, section, description, passed, expected, computed, rel_error, method}`).
* Running `python verify/verify_all.py` executes every check under Python 3.10.4 (`numpy`, `scipy`, `sympy`), prints a console summary, writes `verify/verification_report.json` and `verify/verification_summary.md`, and exits `0` iff 100% of checks pass.
