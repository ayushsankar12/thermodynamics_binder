## 2027 Scoring Engine, Penalty Stacking & Tiebreaker Mathematics

### [FORMULA & DECISION CARD: Official 2027 Scoring Equations, Penalty Multipliers & Tiebreakers (Rule 6.a–j & Scoresheet v2.0.0 { **[S01,S04]**})]

All temperature inputs and calculations are strictly in **degrees Celsius ($°C$)** (`Rule 6.b`). High score wins ($\max \mathrm{FS} = 100.0\text{ pts}$):

$$
\begin{aligned}

  \mathrm{FS} = \mathrm{ES} + \mathrm{TS} + \mathrm{PS}, \quad
  \mathrm{ES} = 50 \times \frac{\text{Part I Score}}{\text{Highest Part I}}, \quad
  \mathrm{TS} = 30 \times M_{\mathrm{pen}} \times \frac{\mathrm{TG}}{\text{Highest }\mathrm{TG}}, \quad
  \mathrm{PS} = 20 \times M_{\mathrm{pen}} \times \frac{\mathrm{PE}}{\text{Highest }\mathrm{PE}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}

  \mathrm{TG} = \max\!\left(0,\; T_f - T_i\right)\ [°C], \qquad
  \mathrm{PE} = \max\!\left(0,\; 1 - \frac{\left|T_f - T_{\mathrm{pred}}\right|}{T_f}\right)\ \left(T \text{ in }°C\right), \qquad
  M_{\mathrm{pen}} = (0.7)^{V_c} \times (0.9)^{V_p}
\end{aligned}
$$
- **Construction Violations ($V_c$, $\times 0.7$ each to $\mathrm{TS},\mathrm{PS}$, Rule `6.h` & Box 3):** Unfixed violations at end of 3-min Setup across `2.d` (non-impound/temp  **[S02]**), `3.b.ii` (probes/thermoses/coolers/vacuum), `3.c` (9 oz cup fit), `3.d` ($30.0 cm$ cube), `3.e` (water contact/additives), `3.g` (room temp), `3.h` (Building Policy).
- **Competition Violations ($V_p$, $\times 0.9$ each to $\mathrm{TS},\mathrm{PS}$, Rule `6.g` & Box 5):** Violations of `5.II.b` (post-setup modification beyond cup insert/remove) or `5.II.d` (altering cup/water, tape/adhesives/clips on cup  **[S02]**, or swirling/stirring water  **[S02]**).
- **Official Tiebreakers (`Rule 6.j`  **[S04]**):** **1st (`TB1`): Best $\mathrm{TS}$**; **2nd (`TB2`): Best $\mathrm{ES}$**; **3rd (`TB3`): Preselected written test questions**.

### Worked Example — Clean Run vs. Stacked Penalty Audit on a 55-Team Invitational Field `[2027 Rule 6.c–h]`

At an invitational where field highs are $\max(\text{Part I}) = 85.00\text{ pts}$ (out of $238.0$, Cornell 2027 `E1`  **[S25]**), $\max(\mathrm{TG}) = 16.0 °C$, and $\max(\mathrm{PE}) = 0.9950$, **Team Alpha** scores $78.20\text{ pts}$ on Part I, starts at $T_i = 21.4 °C$, predicts $T_{\mathrm{pred}} = 36.3 °C$, and reaches $T_f = 36.6 °C$ ($V_c = V_p = 0$). **Team Beta** achieves identical raw numbers but failed to impound a spare lid ($V_c = 1$, `2.d`  **[S02]**) and taped cling wrap to the cup ($V_p = 1$, `5.II.d`  **[S02]**). Compute both Final Scores.
**Solution.** For **Team Alpha** ($M_{\mathrm{pen}} = 1.0000$): $\mathrm{ES}_\alpha = 50(78.20/85.00) = 46.0000\text{ pts}$, $\mathrm{TG}_\alpha = 36.6 - 21.4 = 15.2 °C \implies \mathrm{TS}_\alpha = 30(15.2/16.0) = 28.5000\text{ pts}$, and $\mathrm{PE}_\alpha = 1 - 0.3/36.6 = 0.991803 \implies \mathrm{PS}_\alpha = 20(0.991803/0.9950) = 19.9357\text{ pts}$, giving $\mathrm{FS}_\alpha = 46.0000 + 28.5000 + 19.9357 = \mathbf{94.4357 \approx 94.44\text{**pts**}}$.
For **Team Beta**, $M_{\mathrm{pen},\beta} = (0.7)^1(0.9)^1 = 0.6300$ multiplies $\mathrm{TS}$ and $\mathrm{PS}$ ($\mathrm{ES}_\beta = 46.0000$ unchanged): $\mathrm{TS}_\beta = 28.5000(0.63) = 17.9550\text{ pts}$ and $\mathrm{PS}_\beta = 19.935744(0.63) = 12.5595\text{ pts}$, yielding $\mathrm{FS}_\beta = \mathbf{76.5145 \approx 76.51\text{**pts**}}$ (a loss of $\mathbf{17.92\text{**overall points**}}$!).

### Worked Example — Tiebreaker Resolution (`TB1 = Best TS`) & The Celsius Denominator in $\mathrm{PE}$ `[2027 Rule 6.b, 6.f, 6.j]`

(a) **Team Gamma** ($\mathrm{ES}=44.00, \mathrm{TS}=29.00, \mathrm{PS}=18.50$) and **Team Delta** ($\mathrm{ES}=45.50, \mathrm{TS}=27.50, \mathrm{PS}=18.50$) tie at $\mathrm{FS} = 91.50\text{ pts}$. Who wins? (b) At $T_f = 32.0 °C$ ($\max\mathrm{PE}=1.000, M_{\mathrm{pen}}=1$), how many $\mathrm{PS}$ points does a $0.40 °C$ prediction error cost, and by what factor does a Kelvin denominator misestimate it?
**Solution.** (a) Under Rule `6.j`, **1st Tiebreaker (`TB1`) is Best $\mathrm{TS}$**, *not* Best $\mathrm{ES}$. Because $\mathrm{TS}_\gamma = 29.00 > \mathrm{TS}_\delta = 27.50$, **Team Gamma wins the tie** despite a lower written score.
(b) In Celsius (`Rule 6.b`), $\Delta\mathrm{PE} = |\Delta T_{\mathrm{err}}|/T_f = 0.40/32.0 = 0.01250$, costing $\Delta\mathrm{PS} = 20(0.01250) = \mathbf{0.250\text{**pts**}}$. Using $T_f = 305.15 K$ gives $\Delta\mathrm{PE}_K = 0.001311$, underestimating point sensitivity by $305.15/32.0 = \mathbf{9.536\times}$.
