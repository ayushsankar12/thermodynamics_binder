\section{Comprehensive Thermodynamics \& 2027 Device Glossary (A--Z)}\label{app:C.2}
{\scriptsize
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=0.5pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item[Absolute zero ($\SI{0}{\kelvin} = \SI{-273.15}{\celsius}$):]\idxtopic{absolute zero} Lower bound of thermodynamic temperature where classical kinetic energy vanishes and crystal entropy reaches its Third-Law floor ($S \to 0$).
  \item[Acentric factor ($\omega$):]\idxtopic{acentric factor} Pitzer non-sphericity metric $\omega = -\log_{10}(P_{\mathrm{sat}}/P_c)_{T_r=0.7} - 1.0$ measuring departure from simple spherical-molecule corresponding states.
  \item[Adiabatic process / wall:]\idxtopic{adiabatic process} Process or boundary with zero heat transfer ($Q = 0$); a reversible adiabatic process is \emph{isentropic} ($\Delta S = 0$, $PV^\gamma = \mathrm{const}$ for ideal gas).
  \item[Amagat's law of additive volumes:]\idxtopic{Amagat's law} Total volume of an ideal-gas mixture at $(T,P)$ equals the sum of component partial volumes $V_i = x_i V$.
  \item[Antoine equation:]\idxtopic{Antoine equation}\idxformula{Antoine vapor pressure $\log_{10}P_{\mathrm{sat}} = A - B/(T+C)$} Empirical 3-parameter vapor-pressure correlation $\log_{10} P_{\mathrm{sat}} = A - B/(T + C)$ derived from Clausius--Clapeyron.
  \item[Available energy (Exergy, $X$):]\idxtopic{exergy (available energy)}\idxformula{Gouy-Stodola lost work $X_{\mathrm{dest}} = T_0 S_{\mathrm{gen}}$} Maximum useful work obtainable as a system comes into equilibrium with the ambient dead state $(T_0, P_0)$; destroyed exergy is $X_{\mathrm{dest}} = T_0 S_{\mathrm{gen}} \ge 0$.
  \item[Avogadro's law:]\idxtopic{Avogadro's law} Equal volumes of all ideal gases at the same temperature and pressure contain equal numbers of molecules ($V/n = RT/P$).
  \item[Biot number ($\mathrm{Bi} = hL_c/k_s$):]\idxtopic{Biot number}\idxerr{Biot vs Nusselt conductivity swap ($k_{\mathrm{solid}}$ vs $k_{\mathrm{fluid}}$)} Ratio of internal solid conduction resistance to external boundary convection resistance; $\mathrm{Bi} < 0.1$ validates lumped-capacitance $T(t)$.
  \item[Blackbody:]\idxtopic{blackbody radiation} Idealized perfect absorber ($\alpha_\lambda = 1$) and emitter ($\varepsilon_\lambda = 1$) at all wavelengths and directions ($M_b = \sigma T^4$).
  \item[BoPET selective glazing (Mylar):]\idxtopic{BoPET glazing film}\idxdev{BoPET film unattached lid (FAQ 10/21/2026)} Biaxially oriented polyethylene terephthalate film with high near-IR transmittance ($\tau_{\mathrm{NIR}} \approx 0.90$) used as an unattached cup vapor barrier (FAQ 10/21/2026 on items on the cup).
  \item[Boyle temperature ($T_B$):]\idxtopic{Boyle temperature}\idxformula{Boyle temperature $T_B = a/(Rb) = (27/8)T_c$} Temperature where the second virial coefficient vanishes ($B(T_B) = 0$, so $Z \approx 1$ as $P\to 0$; for a van der Waals gas, $T_B = a/(Rb) = \tfrac{27}{8}T_c$).
  \item[Calorimeter constant ($C_{\mathrm{cal}}$):]\idxtopic{calorimeter heat capacity} Effective heat capacity ($\si{\joule\per\kelvin}$) of the calorimeter cup, stirrer, and thermometer probe assembly ($Q_{\mathrm{cal}} = C_{\mathrm{cal}}\Delta T$).
  \item[Carnot theorem \& efficiency:]\idxtopic{Carnot theorem} No heat engine operating between $T_H$ and $T_C$ can exceed $\eta_C = 1 - T_C/T_H$ (\si{\kelvin}), and all reversible engines between $T_H, T_C$ have $\eta = \eta_C$.
  \item[Choked / critical insulation radius ($r_{\mathrm{crit}}$):]\idxtopic{critical radius of insulation}\idxerr{Critical radius cylinder ($k/h$) vs sphere ($2k/h$) swap} Outer insulation radius $r_{\mathrm{crit}} = k_{\mathrm{ins}}/h_{\mathrm{ext}}$ (cylinder) or $2k_{\mathrm{ins}}/h_{\mathrm{ext}}$ (sphere) that maximizes heat loss.
  \item[Clausius inequality:]\idxtopic{Clausius inequality}\idxformula{Clausius inequality $\oint \delta Q/T \le 0$} $\oint \delta Q/T_b \le 0$ for any cycle, with equality iff totally reversible; implies $dS \ge \delta Q/T$.
  \item[Clausius--Clapeyron equation:]\idxtopic{Clausius-Clapeyron equation} Phase-boundary slope $dP/dT = L/(T\Delta v) \approx P\Delta H_{\mathrm{vap},m}/(RT^2)$ when $v_g \gg v_f$ and vapor behaves as an ideal gas.
  \item[Coefficient of performance ($\mathrm{COP}$):]\idxtopic{coefficient of performance (COP)}\idxerr{COP refrigerator vs heat pump off-by-one ($\mathrm{COP}_{\mathrm{HP}} = \mathrm{COP}_R + 1$)} Ratio of desired heat transfer to work input: $\mathrm{COP}_R = Q_C/W_{\mathrm{in}}$ (cooling) and $\mathrm{COP}_{\mathrm{HP}} = Q_H/W_{\mathrm{in}} = \mathrm{COP}_R + 1$ (heating).
  \item[Colligative properties:]\idxtopic{colligative properties} Solution properties ($\Delta T_b = i K_b m$, $\Delta T_f = -i K_f m$, osmotic pressure $\Pi = i c R T$, $c$ = molarity) that depend on total solute particle concentration $i m$.
  \item[Compressibility factor ($Z = PV/nRT$):]\idxtopic{compressibility factor Z} Measure of non-ideality ($Z=1$ ideal gas; $Z<1$ attractive forces dominate; $Z>1$ repulsive excluded volume $b$ dominates; $Z_c = 3/8 = 0.375$ for vdW gas).
  \item[Compound parabolic concentrator (CPC):]\idxtopic{compound parabolic concentrator (CPC)}\idxdev{compound parabolic concentrator (CPC) vs conical reflector} Non-imaging reflector profile achieving the thermodynamic sine-law concentration limit $C_{\max} = 1/\sin^2\theta_a$ (3D) without tracking.
  \item[Contact conductance ($h_c$):]\idxtopic{contact conductance} Interfacial conductance $h_c = q''/\Delta T_{\mathrm{interface}}$ across micro-asperities between mating solids ($R_{\mathrm{contact}} = 1/(h_c A)$).
  \item[Critical point ($T_c, P_c, V_{m,c}$):]\idxtopic{critical point}\idxfig{Water and CO2 PT phase diagrams with triple and critical points} Terminus of the liquid--vapor coexistence curve where liquid and vapor phases become indistinguishable ($\Delta H_{\mathrm{vap}} \to 0$).
  \item[Curzon--Ahlborn efficiency ($\eta_{\mathrm{CA}}$):]\idxtopic{Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency}\idxformula{Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency $\eta_{\mathrm{CA}} = 1 - \sqrt{T_C/T_H}$} Efficiency $\eta_{\mathrm{CA}} = 1 - \sqrt{T_C/T_H}$ of an endoreversible heat engine operating at maximum power output under finite-rate heat transfer.
  \item[Dalton's law of partial pressures:]\idxtopic{Dalton's law} Total pressure of an ideal-gas mixture equals the sum of component partial pressures $P_i = x_i P$.
  \item[Dead-air gap ($\delta_c \approx \SI{11}{\milli\meter}$):]\idxtopic{dead-air gap insulation}\idxdev{dead-air cavity thickness exceeding 12 mm convection onset} Enclosed air space thin enough ($\mathrm{Ra}_\delta < 1708$, $\delta \lesssim \SI{11}{\milli\meter}$) that buoyancy cannot initiate convection cells, so $\mathrm{Nu}_\delta = 1$.
  \item[Debye $T^3$ law \& Dulong--Petit limit:]\idxtopic{Dulong-Petit law}\idxtopic{Debye T3 law} Lattice heat capacity approaches $C_{V,m} \to 3R = \SI{24.94}{\joule\per\mole\per\kelvin}$ per mole of atoms for $T \gtrsim \Theta_D$, and falls as $C_{V,m} \propto (T/\Theta_D)^3$ as $T \to 0\,\si{\kelvin}$.
  \item[Delisle scale ($^\circ\mathrm{De}$):]\idxtopic{Delisle temperature scale}\idxerr{Delisle inverted temperature scale sign trap} Historical \emph{inverted} scale ($0\,^\circ\mathrm{De}$ at $\SI{100}{\celsius}$ steam point, $150\,^\circ\mathrm{De}$ at $\SI{0}{\celsius}$ ice point): $T_{\mathrm{De}} = 150 - 1.5\,T_C$.
  \item[Dew-point temperature ($T_{\mathrm{dp}}$):]\idxtopic{dew point temperature} Temperature at which moist air cooled at constant pressure and humidity ratio reaches saturation ($P_v = P_{\mathrm{sat}}(T_{\mathrm{dp}})$).
  \item[Diathermal wall:]\idxtopic{diathermal wall} Boundary that permits heat transfer without mass transfer; two systems separated by a diathermal wall reach thermal equilibrium ($T_A = T_B$).
  \item[Eutectic point ($T_E, w_E$):]\idxtopic{eutectic phase diagram}\idxfig{Binary eutectic phase diagram and lever-rule tie-line} Minimum-melting invariant point ($F=0$ at fixed $P$) in a binary system where liquid transforms isothermally into two solid phases ($L \rightleftharpoons \alpha + \beta$).
  \item[Extensive vs.\ intensive properties:]\idxtopic{extensive vs intensive properties}\idxerr{Dividing two extensive properties gives an intensive property} Extensive quantities ($U, H, S, G, V, m, n, C$) scale linearly with system size; intensive quantities ($T, P, \rho, c, v, \mu_i$) are size-independent. The ratio of any two extensive properties is intensive!
  \item[Fin efficiency ($\eta_f$) \& effectiveness ($\varepsilon_f$):]\idxtopic{fin efficiency and effectiveness} $\eta_f = \dot{Q}_{\mathrm{fin}}/\dot{Q}_{\max} = \tanh(mL_c)/(mL_c)$ compares actual fin heat transfer to an isothermal fin at base temp $T_b$; $\varepsilon_f = \dot{Q}_{\mathrm{fin}}/(h A_c \Delta T_b)$ must exceed $2$ to justify fins.
  \item[Fourier's law of conduction:]\idxtopic{Fourier's law of conduction} Conductive heat flux vector $\mathbf{q}'' = -k\nabla T$ (\si{\watt\per\square\meter}) is proportional to the negative temperature gradient.
  \item[Gibbs phase rule ($F = C - P + 2$):]\idxtopic{Gibbs phase rule}\idxformula{Gibbs phase rule $F = C - P + 2$} Number of independent intensive variables $F$ for $C$ chemically independent components across $P$ coexisting phases in equilibrium ($F = C - P + 1$ at fixed pressure).
  \item[Gibbs paradox:]\idxtopic{Gibbs paradox} Classical distinguishable-particle formula predicts $\Delta S = 2nR\ln 2 > 0$ when mixing two identical gases at $(T,P)$; resolved by quantum indistinguishability ($1/N!$), giving $\Delta S = 0$.
  \item[Gray vs.\ selective surface:]\idxtopic{selective solar absorber}\idxfig{Two-band spectral selectivity curve vs blackbody lamp and water spectra} A gray surface has wavelength-independent $\alpha = \varepsilon$; a selective absorber has high near-IR absorptance ($\alpha_{\mathrm{NIR}} \gtrsim 0.90$) and low thermal-IR emissivity ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{MIR}} \lesssim 0.15$).
  \item[Heat-engine sign \& orientation rule:]\idxtopic{PV and TS cycle orientation}\idxfig{Carnot, Otto, Diesel, and Brayton cycles on PV and TS planes} Clockwise closed loop on $P$--$V$ or $T$--$S$ has $W_{\mathrm{net}} = \oint P\,dV = \oint T\,dS > 0$ (power cycle); counterclockwise loop has $W_{\mathrm{net}} < 0$ (refrigerator / heat pump).
  \item[Inversion temperature ($T_{\mathrm{inv}}$):]\idxtopic{Joule-Thomson inversion temperature}\idxerr{Joule-Thomson throttling warms H2 and He at 300 K} Locus where $\mu_{\mathrm{JT}} = (\partial T/\partial P)_H = 0$ ($T_{\mathrm{inv,max}} = 2a/(Rb) = \tfrac{27}{4}T_c$ for vdW gas). Since $\mathrm{He}$ ($T_{\mathrm{inv}}\approx\SI{40}{\kelvin}$) and $\mathrm{H_2}$ ($\approx\SI{202}{\kelvin}$) have $T_{\mathrm{inv}} < \SI{300}{\kelvin}$, throttling them at room temperature \emph{warms} them!
  \item[Kirchhoff's radiation law: ]\idxtopic{Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation} Spectral directional emissivity equals spectral directional absorptance at the same wavelength and temperature: $\varepsilon_\lambda(T) = \alpha_\lambda(T)$.
  \item[Lever rule: ]\idxtopic{binary eutectic lever rule} In any binary two-phase region $(\alpha + L)$, the mass fraction of each phase equals the opposite tie-line arm divided by the total tie-line length: $f_L = (w_0 - w_\alpha)/(w_L - w_\alpha)$.
  \item[Log-mean temperature difference ($\Delta T_{\mathrm{lm}}$):]\idxtopic{log-mean temperature difference (LMTD)}\idxformula{LMTD $\Delta T_{\mathrm{lm}} = (\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2)/\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)$} Exact mean driving temperature difference in a steady-flow heat exchanger: $\Delta T_{\mathrm{lm}} = (\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2)/\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)$.
  \item[Lumped-capacitance model:]\idxtopic{lumped capacitance transient conduction} Exponential heating/cooling law $\theta(t)/\theta_i = \exp(-t/\tau)$ with $\tau = \rho c_p V/(h A_s) = R_{\mathrm{th}}C$, valid when $\mathrm{Bi} = hL_c/k_s < 0.10$.
  \item[Maxwell--Boltzmann speed ratios:]\idxtopic{Maxwell-Boltzmann speed distribution}\idxformula{Gas speeds $v_{\mathrm{mp}} : \bar{v} : v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{2} : \sqrt{8/\pi} : \sqrt{3}$} For an ideal gas at temperature $T$, $v_{\mathrm{mp}} = \sqrt{2RT/M} < \bar{v} = \sqrt{8RT/(\pi M)} < v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{3RT/M}$ in ratio $1 : 1.1284 : 1.2247$.
  \item[Polystyrene / \#6 PS Disqualification Rule:]\idxtopic{banned materials (Rule 3.b.i polystyrene/Styrofoam)}\idxdev{Polystyrene / Styrofoam / number-6 PS disqualification hazard} Under 2027 Rule \texttt{3.b.i} and FAQ 10/22/2026 on polystyrene, ANY polystyrene (expanded EPS Styrofoam, extruded XPS board, or rigid \texttt{\#6 PS} plastic cup/sheet) triggers immediate \textbf{Part II Disqualification}.
  \item[Prediction Score ($\mathrm{PS}$) Celsius Rule:]\idxtopic{Prediction Score (PS) formula}\idxerr{Kelvin vs Celsius in 2027 PE denominator ($T_f$ must be in C)} Under 2027 Rule \texttt{6.b}, $\mathrm{PE} = 1 - |T_f - T_{\mathrm{pred}}|/T_f$ uses $T_f$ in \textbf{degrees Celsius} (never Kelvin!).
  \item[State vs.\ path functions:]\idxtopic{state functions vs path functions} State functions ($P, T, V, U, H, S, F, G$) depend only on equilibrium state endpoints ($dU$ exact); path functions ($Q, W_{\mathrm{by}}, S_{\mathrm{gen}}$) depend on process trajectory ($\delta Q, \delta W$ inexact).
