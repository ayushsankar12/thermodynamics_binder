## 1. Clause-by-Clause Extraction of `THERMODYNAMICS C` (Manual pp. C57–C59, Paraphrased with Minimal Exact Terms)

### Header & Administrative Metadata (`p. C57`)
- **Event Classification:** **Hybrid Event** (upper-right icon on p. C57 corresponds to *Hybrid Event* on p. C2: combines a Core Knowledge written test with a pre-built device tested on site).
- **Team Size:** Up to **2** participants (`A TEAM OF UP TO: 2`).
- **Approximate Event Duration:** **50 minutes** (`APPROXIMATE TIME: 50 minutes`).
- **Impound Required:** **Yes** (`IMPOUND: Yes`).
- **Permitted Calculator Level:** **Class III** (`CALCULATOR: Class III`).
- **Eye Protection Requirement:** **None listed** in the event header (and confirmed omitted on `2027ThermodynamicsBC_checklist.pdf`; per `p. C75`, unpressurized water without hazardous additives is not a hazardous liquid and does not require protective eyewear).

### Section 1 — Description (`1`)
- Teams pre-construct a device prior to the tournament `"designed to collect heat"` and complete a written test covering thermodynamic concepts.

### Section 2 — Event Parameters (`2.a`–`2.e`)
- **`2.a` (Writing Utensils):** Each team may bring writing utensils.
- **`2.b` (Calculators):** Each team may bring up to **two Class III calculators** (or lower classes I/II per `p. C74`) for use during **any part of the event** (both Part I Written Test and Part II Device Testing/Prediction calculations).
- **`2.c` (Notes & Paper Resources — UNRESTRICTED FORMAT & QUANTITY):**
  - Each team may bring a collection of notes and resources **written or printed on paper, of any size**, containing information **in any form and from any source**.
  - **Permitted containers/accessories:** Binders, books, notebooks, folders, sheet protectors, lamination, tabs, and labels are explicitly permitted.
  - **Page removal explicitly allowed:** Participants are responsible for organizing and containing their notes efficiently and **may separate or remove pages from containers for use during any part of the event** (unlike events that require notes to remain secured inside a closed 3-ring binder).
  - **Not impounded:** Paper notes/binders (`2.c`), calculators (`2.b`), writing utensils (`2.a`), setup tools (`2.d`), and prepared calculation graphs/tables (`2.d`) are **NOT required to be impounded** (`2.d` specifies `"Only the device and spare parts must be impounded"`).
- **`2.d` (Device, Spare Parts, Tools, Graphs/Tables & Impound Boundary):**
  - Each team may bring **one pre-constructed device**, **spare parts**, **tools** (explicitly defined as `"items aiding setup, not part of the build"`), and **any prepared graphs or tables** for use in calculating the device's results (temperature prediction).
  - **Strict Impound Rule:** **Only the device and spare parts must be impounded**, and **all impounded items must be at room temperature when impounded**.
  - **Consequence of Failing to Impound (Official Clarification `10/28/2026` & Checklist `2.d` / Scoresheet v2.0.0):** Failure to impound the device/spare parts (or impounding items not at room temperature) incurs **one additional construction parameter violation** ($\times 0.7$ multiplier applied to $\text{TS}$ and $\text{PS}$). Per Checklist `2.d`, supervisors do not inspect for other construction violations during impound itself (those are checked at Check-In/Setup).
- **`2.e` (Supervisor-Provided Testing Materials):**
  - Event Supervisors provide all testing apparatus listed in Section 4 (`COMPETITION AREA`). Teams must **not** bring those testing materials.

### Section 3 — Construction Parameters (`3.a`–`3.h`)
- **`3.a` (Functional Objective):** The device is designed to **collect heat** from an Event Supervisor-provided lamp setup in order to raise the temperature of room-temperature water.
- **`3.b` (Prohibited Materials & Components):** Devices may be built from and contain anything **except**:
  - **`3.b.i` (Hazardous Materials):** Hazardous materials, including but not limited to **asbestos, mineral wool, and fiberglass**.
    - **Binding Expansion via Official Clarification (`10/22/2026`, Sec. 3 / Par. i):** **Polystyrene (including Styrofoam and plastics bearing recyclable symbol `#6` / `PS`)** is officially classified as hazardous in high-heat lamp situations and is **prohibited under `3.b.i`**.
  - **`3.b.ii` (Prohibited Thermal/Insulating Commercial Gear):** **Thermometers, probes, commercially available thermoses, coolers, or vacuum-sealed devices**.
  - **`3.b.iii` (Mandatory Device Exclusion for `3.b.i` Hazardous Materials):** If a team uses any hazardous material prohibited by `3.b.i` (asbestos, mineral wool, fiberglass, or polystyrene/Styrofoam/`#6 PS`), the device **will not be allowed to be set up, modified, or tested** ($\text{TS} = 0, \text{PS} = 0$). Such teams are **only allowed to compete in Part I ( the written test)**.
- **`3.c` (Cup Insertion & Removal):** The device must allow participants to insert and remove an Event Supervisor-provided **9 oz disposable plastic party cup** filled with room-temperature water.
- **`3.d` (Maximum Cubic Bounding Envelope):** When set up for testing, the entire device **including the cup of water** must fit within a **$\mathbf{30.0\text{ cm} \times 30.0\text{ cm} \times 30.0\text{ cm}}$ cube**.
- **`3.e` (Internal Overhang vs. Zero Water Contact / Zero Additives):**
  - Parts of the device **may extend inside the cup** (e.g., an internal upper radiative cone/baffle above the water line), **provided NO part of the device contacts the water**.
  - **Nothing may be added to the water** (no dyes, salts, surfactants, phase-change pellets, or submerged absorber plates).
- **`3.f` (Zero Internal Energy Sources & Post-Test Disassembly Inspection):**
  - Devices must contain **no energy sources** (e.g., no electrical components, battery-powered heaters, chemical reactions, exothermic salts, etc.) to assist in warming the water.
  - At the Event Supervisor's discretion, teams **must disassemble their devices at the end of the testing period** to verify construction materials.
  - **Severe Penalty:** Use of any energy source prohibited by `3.f` results in **$\text{TS} = 0$ and $\text{PS} = 0$** (confirmed in `6.e.iii`, `6.f.iii`, and Checklist Box 2).
- **`3.g` (Room-Temperature Start Condition):** No part of the device may be significantly different from room temperature at the start of the event.
- **`3.h` (Student Construction & Building Policy Compliance):** Competitors must be able to answer Event Supervisor questions regarding the design, construction, and operation of the device in accordance with the official Science Olympiad Building and Tools Policy on `www.soinc.org`.

### Section 4 — Competition Area (`4.a`–`4.g`)
- **`4.a` (Lamp & Bulb Specification):** For each testing station, the Event Supervisor provides a **clamp lamp with a metal reflector** fitted with a **100–125 watt heat lamp bulb**.
  - **Bulb Restrictions:** The bulb **must not be tinted** (no red/tinted brooder bulbs) and **may not be LED, halogen, or ceramic**. (Standard clear/untinted 100 W or 125 W incandescent/IR reflector heat lamp bulb, e.g., BR40/R40 clear heat lamp).
- **`4.b` (Mounting & Testing Surface):** The lamp is mounted **facing straight down** above the **Testing Surface** (table or countertop where the device rests). Multiple identical lamp setups may be operated concurrently.
- **`4.c` (Bulb Height Tiers Above Testing Surface):**
  - The height of the bulb and reflector above the Testing Surface must be identical for all teams in a tournament.
  - Height is measured from the **Testing Surface to the bottommost point of the bulb**:
    - **`4.c.i` Regionals:** **$40\text{–}50\text{ cm}$**, in **$5\text{ cm}$ increments** (i.e., $40, 45,\text{ or } 50\text{ cm}$).
    - **`4.c.ii` States:** **$40\text{–}55\text{ cm}$**, in **$5\text{ cm}$ increments** (i.e., $40, 45, 50,\text{ or } 55\text{ cm}$).
    - **`4.c.iii` Nationals:** **$40\text{–}65\text{ cm}$**, in **$1\text{ cm}$ increments** (any integer centimeter from $40\text{ cm}$ to $65\text{ cm}$).
    - *(Note: Notice that even at $h_{\text{bulb}} = 40\text{ cm}$, a max-height $30.0\text{ cm}$ device has at least $10.0\text{ cm}$ of vertical air gap below the bottommost point of the bulb.)*
- **`4.d` (Centering & Stand Foot Clearance):**
  - The lamp must be mounted so the team's device can sit **centered directly under the lamp**.
  - Any protruding parts of the lamp stand (such as stand base/feet or vertical post) must be **no closer than $15\text{ cm}$ to the center of the bulb** (matching the $15\text{ cm}$ half-width of a centered $30.0\text{ cm} \times 30.0\text{ cm}$ device footprint, as illustrated in the p. C58 diagram).
- **`4.e` (Source Water Bath):** The Event Supervisor provides a source water bath at **approximately room temperature**, kept as uniform as possible across all teams.
- **`4.f` (Event Supervisor Thermometer Rules):**
  - The Event Supervisor provides the thermometer used for starting and final water temperature measurements.
  - **Permitted ES Thermometer Types:** Traditional non-mercury thermometer, electronic probe thermometer, or non-contact (IR) thermometer capable of reading to **$0.1\ ^\circ\text{C}$** (`"a tenth of a degree Celsius"`).
  - **Same-Thermometer Consistency Rule:** Multiple thermometers may be used across a room, but **for any given team, the exact same thermometer must be used to measure both their starting and final water temperatures** (canceling systematic zero-point bias in $\text{TG} = T_{\text{final}} - T_{\text{start}}$).
  - **No Competitor Access:** Competitors are **not allowed** to use the Event Supervisor's thermometers.
- **`4.g` (Water Volume & 9 oz Party Cup Dimensions):**
  - The Event Supervisor provides **one cup per team** containing **$100\text{ mL}$ of water** drawn from the source water bath.
  - **Cup Specification:** Disposable, **clear plastic, 9 ounce party cup**, approximately **$3.9\text{–}4.0\text{ inches}$ ($9.9\text{–}10.2\text{ cm}$) tall** with a top mouth diameter of approximately **$2.9\text{–}3.0\text{ inches}$ ($7.4\text{–}7.6\text{ cm}$)**.

### Section 5 — The Competition (`5.I` & `5.II`)

#### Part I: Written Test (`5.I.a`–`5.I.d`)
- **`5.I.a` (Purpose):** Assesses the team's knowledge of thermodynamics.
- **`5.I.b` (Units & Significant Figures):** Unless a question specifically requests otherwise, all answers must be reported in **metric units with appropriate significant figures** (governed by the official Science Olympiad Significant Figures Policy on `soinc.org`).
- **`5.I.c` (Mandatory Topic Distribution — Minimum 3 Questions per Area):**
  - Every written test must include **at least 3 questions from each** of the applicable topic areas below (minimum $5 \times 3 = 15$ questions at Regionals; minimum $7 \times 3 = 21$ questions at States/Nationals):
  - **`5.c.i` *(All Levels)*:** Thermodynamic systems, intensive and extensive properties of a system, definition of temperature, the Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics, temperature scales and conversions, definitions of heat-related units.
  - **`5.c.ii` *(All Levels)*:** Phases of matter, phase transitions, phase diagrams, latent and sensible heat, ideal gas law.
  - **`5.c.iii` *(All Levels)*:** Kinds of heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation), thermal conductivity, heat capacity, specific heat.
  - **`5.c.iv` *(All Levels)*:** Thermodynamic processes (e.g., adiabatic, isothermal, isochoric, isobaric), thermodynamic cycles, heat engines, efficiency, and the First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics.
  - **`5.c.v` *(All Levels)*:** Historical perspective of the contributions of the **exact 8 named scientists** to the field of thermodynamics:
    1. **Kelvin** (William Thomson, 1st Baron Kelvin)
    2. **Joseph Black**
    3. **Joule** (James Prescott Joule)
    4. **Carnot** (Nicolas Léonard Sadi Carnot)
    5. **Planck** (Max Planck)
    6. **Clausius** (Rudolf Clausius)
    7. **Boltzmann** (Ludwig Boltzmann)
    8. **Maxwell** (James Clerk Maxwell)
  - **`5.c.vi` *(State/National Only)*:** Radiant exitance, blackbody radiation, the Stefan–Boltzmann law, and the Third Law of Thermodynamics.
  - **`5.c.vii` *(State/National Only)*:** Entropy and enthalpy.
    - *(Note on Div B vs. Div C cross-check: In the licensed 2027 Division C manual `5.c.vi` and `5.c.vii` are both labeled `"State/National only"`. Even at Regional or Invitational tournaments, high-tier Division C tests frequently test entropy/enthalpy/radiation or run State/National rules, so the binder must cover all 7 areas `5.c.i–vii` in full while clearly badging `5.c.vi–vii` as `[STATE/NATIONAL TIER — 5.c.vi / 5.c.vii]`.)*
- **`5.I.d` (Mathematical Scope & Strict Trigonometry/Calculus Prohibition):**
  - **`5.d.i`:** Standard arithmetic operations, including **ratios, exponents, and absolute value**.
  - **`5.d.ii`:** Algebraic manipulations, including **multi-step derivations involving multiple variables**.
  - **`5.d.iii`:** Geometric calculations, including computing the **volume or surface/cross-sectional area of common shapes** (e.g., cylinders, spheres, cubes, truncated cones/frustums for party cups and reflectors).
  - **`5.d.iv`:** **"No questions may require the use of trigonometry or calculus."**
  - **Subtle Boundary — Exponentials & Logarithms (`5.d.i–iv` + `MathGuidelines2022.pdf`):**
    - While **trigonometry and calculus are strictly prohibited** by `5.d.iv`, standard **exponents (`5.d.i`) and algebraic exponential/logarithmic functions** (explicitly listed under Division C Algebra II in `MathGuidelines2022.pdf` p. 2: *"Exponential and logarithmic functions"*) are **fully permitted**!
    - **Practical implication for binder formulas:** All isothermal work ($W = nRT \ln(V_2/V_1)$), entropy changes ($\Delta S = mc \ln(T_2/T_1)$, $\Delta S = nC_V \ln(T_2/T_1) + nR \ln(V_2/V_1)$, $S = k_B \ln \Omega$), Newton's law of cooling/heating exponential decay/approach ($T(t) = T_{\infty} + (T_0 - T_{\infty})e^{-t/\tau}$), and adiabatic power laws ($PV^{\gamma} = \text{const}$, $T V^{\gamma-1} = \text{const}$) must be presented and solved as **closed-form algebraic formulas** using $\ln$, $\exp$, and fractional exponents $x^{\gamma}$ without requiring students to perform differentiation, integration, or trigonometric evaluation on the exam. Where a calculus motivation or view-factor integral is shown for deeper insight, it must be tagged `[SUPPLEMENTARY DERIVATION — closed-form algebraic result below is what you use on the exam per Rule 5.d.iv]`.

#### Part II: Device Testing (`5.II.a`–`5.II.i`)
- **`5.II.a` (Pre-Block Supervisor Announcements):**
  - At the start of each competition block, the Event Supervisor announces (and is recommended to post in written form on the board):
    1. **Bulb height** above the Testing Surface ($h_{\text{bulb}}$ in cm),
    2. **Heating Time** ($t_{\text{heat}}$ in minutes),
    3. **Room temperature** ($T_{\text{room}}$ in $^\circ\text{C}$),
    4. **Source water bath temperature** ($T_{\text{bath}}$ in $^\circ\text{C}$), and
    5. **Bulb specifications** (including **wattage** and **bulb physical measurements/shape**).
  - These parameters must be identical for all teams (except for slight incidental room/bath temperature drift).
  - **Official Heating Time Tiers (`5.II.a.i–ii`):**
    - **`5.II.a.i` Regionals:** **$\mathbf{10\text{ minutes}}$** (fixed).
    - **`5.II.a.ii` States/Nationals:** **$\mathbf{10\text{–}15\text{ minutes}}$**, in **$1\text{ minute}$ increments** ($10, 11, 12, 13, 14,\text{ or } 15\text{ min}$).
- **`5.II.b` (3-Minute Device Setup Time):**
  - Teams receive up to **3 minutes of Device Setup Time** (with the heat lamp **OFF**) to unpack, assemble, set up, or modify their device and begin calculating their final water temperature prediction.
  - Teams **may attempt to fix any construction violations** during this 3-minute window; the 3-minute timer is **not paused** for construction re-checks.
  - **Lockdown after Setup Time:** No modifications may be made to the device after Device Setup Time ends **except for inserting and removing the cup of water**.
  - **Binding Clarifications (`10/22/2026`, Sec. 5 / Par. b):** Lifting/opening a reflector or lid to place the cup underneath and returning the lid to its original position, or opening/closing a door or shifting the device's position/orientation to insert the cup and place it under the lamp, is explicitly part of `"inserting or removing the cup"` and does **not** count as an illegal post-setup modification.
- **`5.II.c` (Water Dispensing & Starting Temperature Measurement):**
  - Using the ES's own graduated cylinder/measuring device and thermometer, the Event Supervisor dispenses **$100\text{ mL}$ of water** into a 9 oz clear plastic party cup for the team, measures and records $T_{\text{start}}$ to the nearest **$0.1\ ^\circ\text{C}$**, and informs the team of $T_{\text{start}}$.
  - Water may be distributed simultaneously or in a staggered schedule across lamp stations.
  - Event Supervisors **will not leave a thermometer inside teams' devices** and **will not measure water temperature while the cup is inside a device**.
- **`5.II.d` (1-Minute Water Setup Time & Removable Wrap Rule):**
  - Upon receiving the cup of water and $T_{\text{start}}$, the team has up to **1 minute of Water Setup Time** (lamp still **OFF**) to insert the cup of water into their device (and center it under the lamp per the ES Guide).
  - Teams **may not alter or manipulate the cup or the water in it** beyond inserting the cup into their device.
  - **Binding Clarification (`10/21/2026`, Sec. 5 / Par. d):** When placing the cup in the device, teams **may place something on or around the cup** (such as plastic cling wrap or foil resting over/around it), **provided**:
    1. **No adhesives, tapes, or fasteners** (such as rubber bands or clips) are applied or attached to the cup,
    2. The **structural integrity and shape of the cup** are not altered,
    3. **No part contacts the water** (`3.e`), and
    4. **All such wrap/foil/covers are completely removed from the cup** before final temperature measurement (`5.II.g` & Official ES Guide).
- **`5.II.e` (Heating Time & Zero-Tolerance No-Touch Rule):**
  - At the end of Water Setup Time, the Event Supervisor turns **ON** the lamp and starts the Heating Time timer.
  - **Strict No-Touch Rule:** The team **may not touch their device or the lamp testing setup during the Heating Time**.
  - **Severe Penalty:** Any team touching their device or the lamp setup during Heating Time receives **$\text{TS} = 0$ and $\text{PS} = 0$** (`5.II.e`, `6.e.iv`, `6.f.iv`, Checklist Box 4).
- **`5.II.f` (Prediction Deadline & Concurrent Written Test Work):**
  - The team must **finalize and submit their prediction of the final water temperature ($T_{\text{predicted}}$, in $^\circ\text{C}$) before the end of the Heating Time**, or they receive **$\text{PS} = 0$** (`5.II.f`, `6.f.iv`, Checklist Box 7). (Per the Official ES Guide, teams are given a notecard and alerted 1 minute before Heating Time expires to hand in their written prediction).
  - Teams may work on the Part I Written Test during the Heating Time at the Event Supervisor's discretion.
- **`5.II.g` (Lamp Off, Cup Removal & 1-Minute Final Temperature Measurement Protocol):**
  - At the end of Heating Time, the Event Supervisor turns **OFF** the lamp.
  - The team must **completely remove the cup of water from their device** and place it on the Testing Surface next to the device.
  - **Complete Separation Rule:** The cup must be **completely separated from all device components** (including removing any unattached foil or plastic cling wrap placed around/over the cup) before the Event Supervisor measures $T_{\text{final}}$.
  - **1-Minute Target Window:** Both the team and the Event Supervisor should attempt to complete cup removal and final temperature measurement **within 1 minute** to minimize post-lamp cooling.
  - **Strict No-Stirring / No-Swirling Protocol (Official Clarifications `09/30/2026`, `10/01/2026`, `10/06/2026` & Official ES Guide):**
    - Neither the students nor the Event Supervisor may stir, swirl, swish, or shake the water before or during temperature measurement.
    - **Thermometer Probe Depth (Official Clarification `10/06/2026` & Official ES Guide):** While the local ES determines the uniform protocol for all teams, the National Rules Committee officially recommends measuring the **top layer of water near the center of the cup**, inserting the probe **only far enough into the upper layer of water to obtain an accurate reading, and no farther**, recording the peak reading to the nearest **$0.1\ ^\circ\text{C}$** before cooling occurs.
    - *(Critical Engineering & Prediction Implication: Because radiative heating from above stratifies the water thermally—making the top layer significantly warmer than the bottom layer—and stirring is strictly forbidden, a device that maximizes top-surface radiative absorption and suppresses evaporative cooling at the top water surface achieves a dramatically higher measured $T_{\text{final}}$ than a well-mixed bulk average calculation would predict!)*
- **`5.II.h` (Scoresheet Data Review):** The Event Supervisor reviews the recorded Part II data on the checklist/scoresheet with the team.
- **`5.II.i` (Appeals Rule):** Any team filing an appeal regarding Part II must leave their device in the competition area (consistent with General Rule 4).

### Section 6 — Scoring (`6.a`–`6.j`) & Official v2.0.0 Scoresheet Formulas
- **`6.a`:** **High Score wins** (maximum possible Final Score = **$100\text{ points}$**).
- **`6.b`:** All scoring calculations are performed in **degrees Celsius ($^\circ\text{C}$)**. *(Crucial: In $\text{PE} = 1 - |T_{\text{final}} - T_{\text{pred}}| / T_{\text{final}}$, $T_{\text{final}}$ in the denominator is in **$^\circ\text{C}$**, NOT Kelvin!)*
- **`6.c` (Final Score Equation):**
  $$\text{FS} = \text{ES} + \text{TS} + \text{PS} \quad (\text{Max } 100\text{ pts})$$
- **`6.d` (Exam Score, max 50 points):**
  $$\text{ES} = 50 \times \frac{\text{Part I Score}}{\text{Highest Part I Score among all teams}}$$
  *(Note: Penalties in `6.g` and `6.h` do NOT multiply $\text{ES}$; only $\text{TS}$ and $\text{PS}$ are multiplied by construction/competition penalty factors.)*
- **`6.e` & `6.g–h` (Temperature Score, max 30 points):**
  - **Temperature Gain ($\text{TG}$):**
    $$\text{TG} = \max\!\left(0,\; T_{\text{final}} - T_{\text{start}}\right)$$
  - **Penalty Multiplier ($M_{\text{pen}}$):**
    $$M_{\text{pen}} = (0.7)^{V_{\text{construction}}} \times (0.9)^{V_{\text{competition}}}$$
    where $V_{\text{construction}}$ is the number of unfixed construction parameter violations at the end of Device Setup Time **including failure to impound** (`2.d`, `3.b.ii`, `3.c`, `3.d`, `3.e`, `3.g`, `3.h`), and $V_{\text{competition}}$ is the number of competition parameter violations (`5.II.b`, `5.II.d`).
  - **Temperature Score ($\text{TS}$):**
    $$\text{TS} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } 3.\text{b.i (hazardous)}, 3.\text{f (energy source)}, 5.\text{II.e (touched during heating)}, \text{or no device (`6.i`)} \\[4pt] 30 \times M_{\text{pen}} \times \dfrac{\text{TG}}{\text{Highest TG of all teams}} & \text{otherwise (if Highest TG} = 0,\text{ ratio} = 1\text{)} \end{cases}$$
- **`6.f` & `6.g–h` (Prediction Score, max 20 points):**
  - **Prediction Estimate ($\text{PE}$):**
    $$\text{PE} = \max\!\left(0,\; 1 - \frac{\left|T_{\text{final}} - T_{\text{predicted}}\right|}{T_{\text{final}}}\right) \quad \left(T \text{ in } ^\circ\text{C}\right)$$
  - **Prediction Score ($\text{PS}$):**
    $$\text{PS} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } 3.\text{b.i}, 3.\text{f}, 5.\text{II.e}, \text{no device (`6.i`), or no prediction submitted before end of Heating Time (`5.II.f`/`6.f.iv`)} \\[4pt] 20 \times M_{\text{pen}} \times \dfrac{\text{PE}}{\text{Highest PE of all teams}} & \text{otherwise (if Highest PE} = 0,\text{ ratio} = 1\text{)} \end{cases}$$
- **`6.i` (Unsafe Operations or No Device):** Teams barred from Part II due to unsafe operations (or `3.b.i` hazardous materials) or that do not bring a device receive $\text{TS} = 0$ and $\text{PS} = 0$, but are still allowed to compete in Part I (Written Test).
- **`6.j` (Official Tiebreakers — Confirmed in Scoresheet v2.0.0 Cells `AS7:AX8`):**
  1. **1st Tiebreaker (`TB1`):** **Best $\text{TS}$** (post-multiplier Temperature Score, column `AK` / `AS`).
  2. **2nd Tiebreaker (`TB2`):** **Best $\text{ES}$** (Exam Score, column `AP` / `AU`).

## 2. General Rules, Official National Policies & Schedule Verification

1. **General Rules (`p. C1`, Rules 1–7 & `https://www.soinc.org/code-ethics-general-rules`):**
   - **Rule 1 (Permitted Unless Excluded):** Actions and items (tools, notes, resources, supplies, electronics, etc.) are permitted unless explicitly excluded in the rules, unsafe, or contrary to the spirit of the problem.
   - **Rule 2 (No External Communication / No Multipurpose Calculators):** Participants may not leave without ES approval or receive external assistance. Devices capable of external communication and calculator apps on multipurpose devices (laptops, phones, tablets, smartwatches) are prohibited unless expressly permitted. Cell phones must be turned off.
   - **Rule 3 (Website Content Binding as Printed Rules):** All Science Olympiad content on `www.soinc.org` (policies, requirements, rule corrections, and rule clarifications/FAQs) **must be treated as if it were included in the printed rules**.
   - **Rule 4 (15-Member Team Construction & Impound Appeals):** All pre-built devices must be constructed, impounded, and operated by one or more of the 15 current team members. Once a device is removed from the event area, appeals related to that device will not be considered.
   - **Rule 5 (Venue Practice):** Practicing at a tournament venue prior to competing is only allowed if the Tournament Director opens the facility to all teams.
   - **Rule 6 (Least Restrictive Penalty & Prompt Notification):** Officials are encouraged to apply the least restrictive penalty for infractions (e.g., per `/scoring-guidelines`, if a device at impound does not meet specs and impound time remains, competitors may modify it into compliance without penalty).
   - **Rule 7 (30-Day Advance Notice of Host Modifications):** State and Regional Tournament Directors must notify teams of any site-dependent rule or modification as early as possible, ideally at least 30 days prior to the tournament.
2. **Calculator Class Descriptions (`p. C74` & `Calculator_Class_Descriptions.pdf`):**
   - **Thermodynamics C allows Class III** (and therefore also Class I and Class II).
   - **Class III** includes stand-alone programmable and/or graphing calculators (e.g., TI-83/84/89/Nspire/Voyage, HP 40/50/Prime, Casio 9750/9850/9860, HP 35S, Casio Super-FX).
   - **Class IV** (calculator apps on laptops, phones, tablets, smartwatches) is strictly prohibited.
   - *(Strategic Note: Because Class III programmable/graphing calculators AND unrestricted paper tables/graphs are both allowed in Thermodynamics C, teams can pre-load a thermal prediction solver program on two TI-84/TI-Nspire/HP Prime calculators AND carry full pre-computed lookup tables in their binder as a zero-failure backup!)*
3. **Significant Figures Policy (`SO SIGNIFICANT FIGURES FINAL12-5-12.pdf` & Rule `5.I.b`):**
   - Measurements include all directly read graduation digits plus **one estimated final digit** (e.g., ES thermometer readings to $0.1\ ^\circ\text{C}$).
   - Standard rounding and propagation: multiplication/division preserves the least number of significant figures; addition/subtraction preserves the least number of decimal places.

|---|---|---|
| **Heat Collector vs. Legacy Insulator** | `1`, `3.a`, `4.a–g`, `5.II.a–g` | Purge all 2018–2019 hot-water-beaker cooling curves and Plot Score references. The 2027 device heats $100\text{ mL}$ of room-temp water in a 9 oz clear plastic cup under a $100\text{–}125\text{ W}$ untinted incandescent/IR heat lamp at $40\text{–}65\text{ cm}$ height for $10\text{–}15\text{ min}$. |
| **Impound Separation Strategy** | `2.c`, `2.d`, Clarification `10/28/2026` | **Impound ONLY the device and spare parts** (open/ventilated at room temperature). Keep the **binder, prepared prediction graphs/tables, calculators, writing utensils, and setup tools** with the competitors so they can refine models and study until their competition block! |
| **Styrofoam / `#6 PS` Hard Disqualification from Part II** | `3.b.i`, `3.b.iii`, Clarification `10/22/2026`, ES Guide | **Never use Styrofoam, expanded polystyrene (EPS), extruded polystyrene (XPS), or `#6 PS` plastic anywhere in the device.** It is classified as a `3.b.i` hazardous material under a heat lamp and results in the device being **barred from setup/testing ($\text{TS}=\text{PS}=0$)**. Use safe alternatives (e.g., cardboard, balsa/basswood, cork, cotton/cellulose, polyisocyanurate/foil-faced board if compliant, air gaps, aluminum sheet/foil, clear acrylic/PET/PP/HDPE films). |
| **Cup Overhang vs. Zero Water Contact** | `3.c`, `3.e`, Clarification `10/21/2026`, `5.II.g` | Internal reflectors/cones may overhang the rim inside the 9 oz cup, and non-adhesive cling wrap or foil may rest over/around the cup (`10/21/2026`), provided **zero adhesives/tape/rubber bands/clips touch the cup**, **nothing touches the water** ($100\text{ mL}$ fills a 9 oz cup less than halfway), and **all wrap/foil is completely removed before $T_{\text{final}}$ is measured**. |
| **Thermal Stratification & Top-Layer Probe Measurement** | `5.II.g`, Clarifications `09/30/2026`, `10/01/2026`, `10/06/2026` (×2), ES Guide | Water is **never stirred or swirled** by students or the ES, and the ES is instructed to measure the **top layer of water near the center of the cup**. Radiative heating from the overhead lamp creates strong vertical thermal stratification ($\Delta T_{\text{top}} > \Delta T_{\text{bulk}}$). Prediction tables (`§10`, `§11`) must include a **stratification factor $\kappa_{\text{strat}} = \Delta T_{\text{top}} / \Delta T_{\text{bulk}} \ge 1.0$** calibrated specifically to top-layer probe depth! |
| **Celsius Denominator in $\text{PE}$** | `6.b`, `6.f.i`, Scoresheet v2.0.0 Cell `AL8` | $\text{PE} = \max(0,\, 1 - |T_{\text{final}} - T_{\text{pred}}| / T_{\text{final}})$ uses $T_{\text{final}}$ in **$^\circ\text{C}$** (typically $22\text{–}35\ ^\circ\text{C}$). A $0.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$ prediction error at $T_{\text{final}} = 25.0\ ^\circ\text{C}$ yields $\text{PE} = 0.9800$ (losing only $2\%$ of $\text{PS}$, or $0.4\text{ pts}$). |
