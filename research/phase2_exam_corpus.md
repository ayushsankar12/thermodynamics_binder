## 3. Question-Level Corpus Matrix (All 8 Exams Mapped to 2027 Syllabus Clauses `5.c.i–vii`)

### 3.1 Legend for 2027 Syllabus Clauses, Question Formats, and Difficulty Tiers

- **2027 Official Division C Syllabus Clauses (`official-2027-C.pdf`, p. C58, Rule `5.I.c.i–vii`):**
  - **`5.c.i` *(All Levels)*:** Thermodynamic systems (open/closed/isolated), intensive & extensive properties, definition of temperature, Zeroth Law of thermodynamics, temperature scales & conversions (K, °C, °F, °R, plus historical scales °De, °N, °Ré, °Rø), definitions of heat-related units (J, cal, kcal, BTU, erg, ft·lbf, eV, CHU, foe).
  - **`5.c.ii` *(All Levels)*:** Phases of matter, phase transitions, phase diagrams (unary $P$-$T$ diagrams for $\text{H}_2\text{O}$, $\text{CO}_2$, Carbon, Ice III; binary alloy eutectic/peritectic/eutectoid diagrams for Al-Cu and Fe-C; Gibbs phase rule; Clapeyron & Clausius–Clapeyron equations), latent & sensible heat, thermal expansion/dilatometry, ideal gas law ($PV=nRT$, Dalton's law, kinetic theory speeds, van der Waals real gas deviations).
  - **`5.c.iii` *(All Levels)*:** Kinds of heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation), thermal conductivity (Fourier's 1D slab and cylindrical shell conduction, Wiedemann–Franz law / Drude electronic transport), Newton's law of cooling/heating, heat capacity & specific heat (calorimetry, method of mixtures, bomb/coffee-cup calorimeters, Mayer's relation $C_{p,m}-C_{v,m}=R$, equipartition degrees of freedom).
  - **`5.c.iv` *(All Levels)*:** Thermodynamic processes (adiabatic, isothermal, isochoric, isobaric, isentropic, free expansion, multi-step paths), thermodynamic cycles (Carnot, Stirling, Rankine, Otto/Brayton/Ericsson), heat engines, efficiency ($\eta_1$, Carnot limit), First & Second Laws of thermodynamics.
  - **`5.c.v` *(All Levels)*:** Historical perspective of thermodynamic scientists — mapped specifically against the **2027 8 Named Scientists** (**Kelvin [William Thomson], Joseph Black, Joule, Carnot, Planck, Clausius, Boltzmann, Maxwell**) while flagging out-of-list historical figures tested in older exams (Galileo, Fahrenheit, Celsius, Amontons, Boyle, Gibbs, Helmholtz, Nernst).
  - **`5.c.vi` *(State/National Only)*:** Radiant exitance, blackbody radiation (Planck curve, Wien shift, Lambertian radiance $L = \sigma T^4/\pi$), Stefan–Boltzmann law ($P = \varepsilon\sigma A T^4$, planetary radiative equilibrium, $T^4$ radiative cooling ODE), and Third Law of thermodynamics ($S \to 0$ and quantum heat-capacity freeze-out $C_v \to 0$ as $T \to 0\text{ K}$).
  - **`5.c.vii` *(State/National Only)*:** Entropy ($\Delta S = \int \delta Q_{\text{rev}}/T$, phase-transition entropy $\Delta S = \Delta H/T$, ideal-gas mixing/expansion entropy, entropy generation $S_{\text{gen}}$) and enthalpy ($\Delta H = \Delta U + \Delta(PV)$, steam tables, Joule–Thomson isenthalpic expansion), plus invitational extensions to Gibbs/Helmholtz free energy ($\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$) and exergy destruction ($X_{\text{dest}} = T_0 S_{\text{gen}}$).
- **Question Format Codes:**
  - `FMT-1`: Multiple Choice & True/False (`MCQ/TF`)
  - `FMT-2`: Short Calculation, Unit & Temperature Scale Conversion (`SHORT-CALC`)
  - `FMT-3`: Multi-Step Calorimetry, Phase Change, or Thermodynamic Cycle Analysis (`MULTI-STEP`)
  - `FMT-4`: PV / TS / Phase / Blackbody Graph & Diagram Reading or Sketching (`GRAPH-DIAG`)
  - `FMT-5`: Experimental Apparatus, Steam Table, or Device Data Analysis (`EXP-DATA`)
  - `FMT-6`: Historical / Scientist Attribution & Short Conceptual Explanation (`HIST-CONCEPT`)
- **Difficulty Tiers:**
  - `Tier 1`: Foundational / Regional Baseline (direct recall, 1-step conversion or plug-in formula)
  - `Tier 2`: Intermediate / State & Standard Invitational (2–3 step synthesis, standard calorimetry/cycle/conduction/entropy calculation)
  - `Tier 3`: Advanced / National & University Invitational Separator (multi-equation derivation, non-standard coordinates, alloy lever rule, steam table construction, exergy, or differential/integral rate laws)

## 6. Point-Losing Traps & Grading Rubric Forensics Across `E1`–`E8`

Direct inspection of the official grading rubrics (`Cornell_2027_Key.pdf`, `MIT_2019_Key.pdf`, `MIT_2018_Thermodynamics_Key.pdf`, `GGSO_2019_Key.pdf`, `GGSO_2018_Key.docx`, `ATX_2018Inv_Key.pdf`, `ATX_2019Reg_Key.pdf`) reveals **10 recurring point-losing traps**:

1. **Significant Figures & Unit Penalty Deductions (10–25% penalty on every numerical problem):**
   - On `E2` (MIT 2019), every numerical calculation awards `1 pt` for numerical value, `0.5 pt` for exact significant figures (3 sig figs), and `0.5 pt` for correct units — meaning **wrong sig figs or missing units costs 25–33% of every problem's points**.
   - On `E4` (GGSO 2019), wrong sig figs (>2 units off in last digit) or missing units costs `-1 pt` per subpart.
   - On `E6` (Cornell 2018), `Sec I` requires 2 sig figs and exact degree notation (`49 °C`, NOT `49 Celsius`), and `Sec V Q3` mandates **1 significant figure** (`8000 J/s`).
   - On `E1` (Cornell 2027), all FRQ answers require **4 significant digits** with zero intermediate rounding.
2. **Gauge vs. Absolute Pressure Trap in Gas Laws & Steam Tables (`E1 Q27b–c, Q28`):**
   - `E1 Q27` gives Marcet boiler pressure as **gauge pressure** ($97.35\text{ kPa}$ at $120\ ^\circ\text{C}$). In the finite-difference Clapeyron slope $\frac{P(T+\Delta T)-P(T-\Delta T)}{2\Delta T}$, $P_{\text{atm}}$ cancels out, so gauge pressures work directly; but in the Ideal Gas Law (`Q27c`) and steam-table saturation pressure (`Q28`), students who forget $P_{\text{abs}} = P_{\text{gauge}} + 101.3\text{ kPa}$ miss `Q27c` and propagate errors into `Q28`!
3. **Celsius vs. Kelvin in Ratios, Logs, and $T^4$ Radiation (`E4 Q14b,d`, `E7 Q19`, `E8 Q26`):**
   - `E8 Q26` ($P_1=700\text{ Torr} \to P_2=800\text{ Torr}$ at $T_1 = 27\ ^\circ\text{C}$) includes distractor `c` ($27 \times 8/7 = 30.9\ ^\circ\text{C}$) specifically to catch teams that fail to convert $27\ ^\circ\text{C} \to 300.15\text{ K}$ before multiplying by $8/7$ ($343\text{ K} = 69.9\ ^\circ\text{C}$).
4. **Incomplete Phase-Transition Checks in Multiphase Calorimetry (`E1 Q30d`, `E3 Q22`, `E4 Q9a`, `E6 Sec III Q2`):**
