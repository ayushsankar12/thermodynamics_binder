## 5. Standard Weekly 50-Minute Mock Block Checklist (Use Every Weekend)

1. **Before Starting Timer (Impound Simulation):**
   - [ ] Device and spare BoPET caps sit open at room temperature for $\ge 30\text{ min}$ (`2.d`, `3.g`).
   - [ ] Partner A & Partner B have Main Binder, QuickRef Booklet, Device Handbook, tear-out `App D`/`App F` worksheets, 2 Class III calculators (`2.b`), pencils/marker (`2.a`), 30 cm ruler, and heat gloves (`2.d`).
2. **Minute `0:00` — ES Block Announcement (`5.II.a`):**
   - [ ] Partner B writes $(P_{\text{bulb}}, h_{\text{bulb}}, t_{\text{heat}}, T_{\text{room}}, T_{\text{bath}})$ at the top of `App D` / `QS-13`.
   - [ ] Partner A begins **Pass 1** of the Written Test (`Tier 1` and `Tier 2` problems in `5.c.i, ii, iv, vii`).
3. **Device Setup Time (`Up to 3:00`, Lamp OFF, `5.II.b`):**
   - [ ] Partner B sets up device, verifies $30.0\text{ cm}$ cube compliance (`3.d`) and zero prohibited items (`3.b`, `3.f`), receives cup of $100\text{ mL}$ water and $T_{\text{start}}$ (`5.II.c`), and computes $T_{\text{predicted}} = T_{\text{start}} + \Delta T_{\text{lookup}}(h_{\text{bulb}}, t_{\text{heat}}, P_{\text{bulb}}, T_{\text{room}} - T_{\text{start}})$.
4. **Water Setup Time (`Up to 1:00`, Lamp OFF, `5.II.d`, `Clarifications #5, #7, #8`):**
   - [ ] Partner B opens reflector lid, places cup smoothly into cradle, rests unattached clear BoPET film cap on cup rim (zero tape/adhesives/clips, zero water contact `3.e`), closes reflector lid, centers device under bulb (`4.d`), and steps back.
5. **Heating Time (`10:00` to `15:00`, Lamp ON, `5.II.e–f`):**
   - [ ] **NEVER TOUCH DEVICE OR LAMP STAND (`5.II.e` — zero tolerance!).**
   - [ ] Partner A verifies Partner B's $T_{\text{predicted}}$ calculation (in $^\circ\text{C}$ to $0.1\ ^\circ\text{C}$); Partner B submits the prediction card at least 5 minutes before Heating Time expires (`5.II.f`).
   - [ ] Partner B solves assigned Written Test sections (`5.c.i, iii, v, vi` + lab/data items) using tear-out binder sections (`2.c`) and the second Class III calculator (`2.b`).
6. **Final Measurement (`Up to 1:00`, Lamp OFF, `5.II.g`, `Clarifications #1–#4`):**
   - [ ] At timer signal (lamp OFF), Partner B opens lid, lifts cup straight out **without swirling, swishing, or shaking**, removes the unattached BoPET cap completely (`5.II.g`), and sets the bare cup on the Testing Surface within $15\text{–}20\text{ seconds}$.
   - [ ] Partner B verifies all recorded values on the ES Checklist (`5.II.h`: 0 violations, $T_{\text{start}}$, $T_{\text{predicted}}$, $T_{\text{final}}$) before returning to finish the last 5 minutes of the Written Test.
