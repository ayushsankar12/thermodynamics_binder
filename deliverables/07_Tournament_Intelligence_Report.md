### 3.2 `RECOMMENDED` Partner Triage & Time-Allocation Protocol (50-Minute 2027 Event Block)

Under the verified 2027 rules (`official-2027-C.pdf`, pp. C58–C59, Section 5 Part II & Official ES Schedule), Part II device testing follows a strict timeline:
- **Device Setup Time:** Up to **3 minutes** (lamp off; configure device for announced bulb height $40\text{--}65\text{ cm}$ and wattage $100\text{--}125\text{ W}$, start prediction calculation).
- **Water Setup Time:** Up to **1 minute** (ES dispenses $100\text{ mL}$ water into $9\text{ oz}$ clear cup and announces $T_{\text{start}}$ to $0.1\ ^\circ\text{C}$; team places non-attached wrap/cover if used per Clarification #5 and inserts cup into device under lamp).
- **Heating Time:** **10 minutes** (Regionals) or **10–15 minutes** (States/Nationals). Team must submit $T_{\text{pred}}$ in °C **before** Heating Time ends (`5.II.f`), and **may work on the Part I written test during Heating Time**!
- **Final Measurement:** Up to **1 minute** (lamp off; team removes cup completely from all device components and places it on Testing Surface; ES measures top water layer near center without stirring/swirling per Clarifications #1–#4).

Because **pages may be separated or removed from binders/containers during any part of the event** (2027 Rule `2.c`), the two competitors should execute a **Parallel Split-Binder Protocol**:

1. **Minutes `0:00 – 4:00` (Setup & Launch):**
